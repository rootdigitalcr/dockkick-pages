DockKick privacy policy

Root Digital · DockKick 1.0.0 · 2 November 2026

DockKick is a local Windows productivity application. It brings together shortcuts to applications and folders, copies the contents of text files you choose, and opens Windows clipboard history.

Information used on your device

DockKick stores the aliases, paths and Windows application identifiers you add, their order, language and theme preferences, and related settings. It reads the text files you select when you request a copy. Windows application names, identifiers, shortcut metadata and icons are used to populate the app picker and show your shortcuts.

The Store edition keeps its configuration and optional user translations in the Windows package's local user-data folder, separately from installed application binaries. Your selected source files and folders stay in their original locations. Changing an alias or removing a DockKick shortcut does not rename, edit or delete the original.

Clipboard

Copying is initiated by you. DockKick writes the selected file's text to the system clipboard and does not paste it automatically. It does not keep its own clipboard-history database. The clipboard history button invokes the native Windows feature. Windows controls that feature, including its enablement, retention and any cloud synchronization you choose in Windows settings.

Network and third parties

DockKick does not upload your selected files, clipboard text, application catalog or configuration to Root Digital. It has no in-app account, advertising SDK or own usage analytics. Its local features do not require an Internet connection.

Opening an application or folder uses Windows and the destination you chose. Those applications, Windows services, your storage provider and Microsoft Store operate under their own settings and privacy terms.

The optional “Support the project” link opens Root Digital on Ko-fi in your browser. The external website handles any visit or contribution under its own privacy and payment terms. DockKick does not collect payment details, and contributing does not unlock app features. Microsoft is not the fundraiser or sponsor.

Your choices and retention

You can change or remove shortcuts and preferences in Settings. Starting with Windows is optional and can also be managed in Windows startup settings. Local settings remain until you change them or Windows removes/resets the app's data. Resetting app data can permanently remove your saved configuration. Changes to data stored or synchronized by Windows or external services are managed with those services.

These documentation pages

These pages are prepared for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub may process technical connection data under its privacy statement. The pages do not include our own analytics or third-party scripts. Support issues on GitHub are public: submit generic examples and redact personal information.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact Root Digital through the public support channel. Do not include passwords, sensitive file contents or an unredacted configuration in a support request. If the application's data practices change, this page will be updated with the corresponding release.
